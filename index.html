Executive Summary

Overcoming Traditional Campus Bottlenecks

In a large institution like ABESIT with multiple academic blocks, cafeterias, and labs, misplaced belongings lead to anxiety and academic disruption. Here is how our centralized system replaces legacy mechanisms.

The Problem: Decentralized & Fragmented Channels

Existing practices in colleges are largely dependent on physical notice boards, handwritten proctor registers, verbal inquiries, and scattered WhatsApp/Telegram messaging groups.

  • Ephemeral Records: Notices are overlooked or torn down; chat messages quickly get buried under daily discussions.
  • No Central Search: Students do not know where to look or who currently holds their misplaced belongings.
  • Unverified Claims: Physical registers lack standardized verification protocols, risking handover to fraudulent claimants.

The Solution: Single-Source Digital Recovery Portal

The Lost and Found Web Portal unifies every misplaced and recovered item into a single, accessible, institutional digital register with automated verification workflows.

  • Structured Reporting: Captures category, campus location, date/time, and photo attachments with privacy safeguards.
  • Transparent Matching Engine: Computes weighted similarity scores without revealing confidential ownership marks.
  • Audited Administrative Oversight: Department custodians approve claims based on private verification proof before closure.

Core System Objectives

Architected to fulfill academic mini-project criteria and institutional operational requirements.

1. Centralized Campus Reporting

Provides students, faculty, and administrative staff with a single canonical web application for reporting lost and found belongings across the ABESIT campus.

2. Structured Item Entry

Enforces structured data capture including category, detailed descriptions, campus zone location, exact timestamps, and optional photograph uploads.

3. Search & Filtering Facilities

Enables multi-criteria query execution by keyword, item category, campus landmark, date range, and current case progression status.

4. Match Suggestions Mechanism

Applies a transparent weighted heuristic comparing categories, keywords, date proximity, and location to surface high-probability candidate matches.

5. Secure Claim & Verification

Guarantees privacy by withholding distinctive identifying features from public view; claimants submit confidential proof reviewed by custodians.

6. Administrator Dashboard

Provides college authorities with tools for content moderation, duplicate resolution, disputed claims arbitration, and transparent digital audit trails.

Intelligent Recovery Engine

Transparent Weighted Matching & Controlled Claims

Rather than relying on opaque black-box machine learning models that can lead to disputed college belongings, the ABESIT portal utilizes a rule-based transparent weighted comparison. This ensures explainable match scores without leaking confidential claimant evidence.

40%

Category Agreement (Weight: 40%)

Strict classification matching (e.g. Electronics to Electronics, IDs to IDs). High baseline differentiator.

25%

Keywords & Description Similarity (Weight: 25%)

Natural language token overlap comparing brand names, colors, models, and physical tags.

20%

Campus Location Proximity (Weight: 20%)

Matches reports filed in the same academic zone (e.g. Library 2nd Flr reading room vs Library desk).

15%

Temporal / Date Proximity (Weight: 15%)

Prefers timestamps occurring on the same day or within 48 hours of reported misplacement.

Ownership Protection Philosophy

Match scores remain advisory indicators. A high percentage does not automatically grant ownership. The claimant must submit non-public verification details (e.g., lock code, internal marks, serial number) for human custodian review.
